Jonah Meadows, Reporter, Editor, Producer - New Orleans

About

Hello world. I am currently North Shore Editor for Patch.com, covering suburban communities Lake and Cook County from Evanston to Lake Forest, Illinois. I am also the creator of the Snow Crash Radio podcast (Subscribe on iTunes) and the former World Editor of Rivet Radio, where I wrote and produced newscasts and audio features for businesses and consumers, and a producer of the former WBEZ program Worldview. I’ve also hosted and produced talk shows in the world of non-commercial radio and created podcasts for businesses and other groups.
